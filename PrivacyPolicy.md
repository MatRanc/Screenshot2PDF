# Privacy Policy for Screenshot2PDF

**Effective date:** August 9, 2026

Screenshot2PDF is designed to be private by default. The short version:
**Screenshot2PDF does not collect, store, or transmit any of your data.
Everything you process stays on your device.**

## Information we collect

**None.** Screenshot2PDF has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising,
and no servers. We do not collect personal information, usage data, or
device identifiers.

## Files and folders

The app is sandboxed and only accesses the folder you explicitly choose via
the system file picker. Cropping and PDF assembly run entirely **on your
Mac**. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## Where your files are stored

The generated PDF (`CroppedOutput.pdf`) is written to the same folder you
selected, on your device. Screenshot2PDF does not keep a separate copy or
send it anywhere.

## Third parties

Screenshot2PDF includes **no third-party SDKs**, advertising networks,
trackers, or analytics. No data is shared with anyone because no data is
collected.

## Network access

Screenshot2PDF's sandbox entitlements request no network access. The app
cannot connect to the internet.

## Children's privacy

Screenshot2PDF does not collect data from anyone, including children, and
contains no ads or external links.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same
location with a new effective date.
